# Contest submission copy

**Project title:** Word Bluff - Can You Spot the Real One?

**Project description:** Word Bluff is a real-time party game where friends join with a room code, invent convincing definitions, and vote for the real one. Each round, a Reader sees the mystery word and clue while everyone else writes a bluff. Earn points for correct guesses, fooling other players, and guiding the group to the truth. Play from a phone or computer with no account or download.
